# Public-domain & free art sources

Use these **first** for reference and, where licenses allow, production assets.

## General

| Source | Notes |
|--------|--------|
| [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/) | Photos, maps; check item rights (many PD) |
| [NYPL Digital Collections](https://digitalcollections.nypl.org/) | NYC streets, buildings; filter by rights |
| [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) | Books, photos, film stills |
| [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) | Filter: public domain / CC0 |
| [Flickr The Commons](https://www.flickr.com/commons) | Institutional PD photos |

## 1930s NYC

- LOC / NYPL street scenes, elevated trains, waterfront, speakeasy exteriors (PD photos)
- Period automobiles, fashion plates in PD publications
- Sanborn fire insurance maps (layout reference; verify rights per sheet)
- Pre-1929 material is usually safer for US PD; 1930s photos may still be under rights — **check each item**

## 1980s NYC

- Most 1980s photography is **still under copyright**
- Prefer: original captures, CC0 packs, purchased licenses, or pure procedural/geometric placeholders
- Do **not** scrape modern stock or game rips

## Implementation rule

1. Prototype with colored primitives / placeholders  
2. Swap in verified PD or original art per asset  
3. Keep a short credit list in-repo for any Commons/LOC item used  

## Audio

- PD historical recordings (e.g. early jazz if PD) or original  
- No commercial tracks without a license
